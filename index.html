📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Basics (All Modes)

🖱️Mouse / Finger: Move your cursor or drag to control your position on screen.
⌨️Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD also move your neon square.
💛Coins (yellow circles) give you +1 Life when collected.
🔑Keys (orange) unlock White Wall obstacles — use a key to slice through them safely.
⚡Power-ups: Green = Slow Motion, Red = Blaster (auto fires bullets).
🌀Portals change your speed temporarily — cyan slows down, magenta speeds up.

♾️ Endless Mode

🟣Dodge pink obstacles falling and flying across the screen.
📈Your Score increases every time you survive and dodge. Obstacles get faster over time!
🏆Your Best Score is saved and shown in the top-right corner.
💀You start with 1 Life. Hit an obstacle and it's Game Over — unless you collected a coin!
⚙️Choose difficulty from Beginner → God. Higher = faster obstacles, more at once.

🏅 Levels Mode

⏳Survive a countdown timer to clear the level. The timer is shown where the score usually is.
⭐Clear a level to earn XP: 1 XP for Level 1, 2 for Level 2... up to 10 XP for Level 10.
🔒Levels are locked by default. Beat a level to unlock the next one automatically.
💎Spend 30 XP to skip a locked level, or 70 XP to unlock all levels at once!
🎉Survive until the timer hits 0 and you win the level — a victory explosion fires off!

🥷 Ninja Mode

✋Click & drag (or swipe on mobile) to draw your glowing green blade across the screen.
🟦Cyan obstacles are fruits — slice them for +1 Score!
💗Pink obstacles are Bombs — slicing them costs you 1 Life. At 1 Life = instant Game Over!
❌If a cyan fruit falls off screen without being sliced, you lose 1 Life.
💛Slice Coins to collect them (+1 Life). Slice Keys to collect them too!
⬜Slice a White Wall with a Key = +1 Score. Without a key = lose 1 Life.
❤️You start with 3 Lives in Ninja Mode — build them up by collecting coins!

🏁 Race Mode

🤖vs CPU: Pick your difficulty and race against AI bots. Last one dodging wins!
🌐Online: Create or join a room with a 4-letter code and race against real players!
💀When you get hit, you're Eliminated — but you can still watch the live standings.
🥇The last surviving player is declared the winner!

❤️‍🔥 Survival Mode

💀You have exactly 1 Life. One hit and it's over — no extra lives, no mercy.
📈The speed ramps up continuously — obstacles never stop getting faster and denser.
⏱️Your score is a stopwatch — every frame you survive is a point. Beat your best time!
💡Tip: Small, controlled movements beat frantic ones. Staying calm at the edges keeps you alive longest.

🌪️ Chaos Mode

⬇️➡️⬆️⬅️Obstacles fall from every direction — top, bottom, left and right. Watch your whole screen!
⚡Power-ups spawn far more often — grab Slow Motion or Blaster whenever you can.
🟣You start with 3 Lives. Coins still give +1 Life each.
💡Tip: In Chaos, the center is a trap. Camp in a corner and react only when something comes your way.

⚡ Speed Rush Mode

⏳You have 60 seconds — the countdown timer shows where your score usually is.
💰Coins are your lifeline: each one adds +1 Life AND +1 point. Grab them all!
🏆When time runs out your final score is saved as your best. Beat it next run!
💡Tip: Stay alive for the full 60s. Dying early wastes the run — prioritize dodging over coin hunting.

☄️ Meteor Mode

⚠️A flashing red warning marker appears before a meteor lands — it shows exactly where impact will happen.
🌠Meteors strike from every edge of the screen, not just the top. Don't hug the walls!
💥After the warning, the meteor slams down and explodes into fragments. Avoid the impact zone.
💡Tip: Watch the warnings, not the meteors. As soon as a marker flashes, move away from it.

🔄 Reverse Mode

🔃Your controls are inverted — ArrowRight moves you LEFT, ArrowUp moves you DOWN. Mouse/finger is flipped too!
🟣Classic Endless obstacles, but your brain has to work backwards to dodge them.
💡Tip: Go slow at first. Move in the "wrong" direction on purpose until the flip becomes muscle memory.

🧘 Zen Mode

🕊️You can't die. Obstacles pass right through you — no lives, no game over, no stress.
💛Chase coins — each one is +1 point. Your goal is a pure, relaxing high score.
🎨Just drift through the neon. It's the perfect mode to chill out and test the skin shop.
💡Tip: No rush! Plan lazy paths through coin lines and watch the obstacles flow by.

💥 Boss Rush Mode

🔫Your blaster is always active — you auto-fire at the boss every few frames. No power-up needed!
💀The boss has an HP bar at the top. Shoot it to chip away its health and end the wave.
🎯Bosses fire aimed shots, radial sprays and dash attacks. Dodge the magenta bullets — each hit costs 1 Life.
👑You start with 3 Lives. Survive all 5 waves of bosses to clear the mode!

🎨 Creator

🖊️Tap the 🎨 Creator button on the main menu to build your own levels — place Blocks, Spikes, Coins, Keys, Portals, Gates, Power-ups, Speed pads and Invert zones on a grid.
🔄Pick Vertical or Side scrolling, set the speed and size, and choose a song to match the vibe of your level.
▶️Hit Playtest to try your level instantly. Press Esc to stop and jump right back into the editor.
📦Save keeps your level on this device. Upload shares it so other players can race through your creation!

💡 Tips & Tricks

1️⃣Always move to the edges of the screen — obstacles are densest in the center.
2️⃣In Ninja Mode, make long diagonal swipes to hit multiple fruits at once.
3️⃣Grab coins early in Endless — the extra life is a huge safety net later.
4️⃣Save your XP to unlock high levels directly if you find Beginner too easy.
5️⃣Slow Motion power-ups let you breathe — use them to collect coins safely.
